Behind the scenes

How your system will be built

A quick, plain-English look at the trusted tools behind your new website and care system. You don’t need to set up or manage any of these. We build everything and look after it for you.

  • Your website and app

    Next.js

    One modern system for your public website, the staff area and the mums’ login area. It’s fast and works on phones, tablets and computers.

  • Secure patient and staff records

    Supabase · stored in London, UK

    Records are kept in the UK and encrypted, with daily backups. Each person only sees what their role allows, and the system keeps a log of who viewed or changed every record.

  • Safe sign-in

    Two-step login

    Staff sign in with a password plus a code from their phone. You’re signed out automatically after a period of inactivity, so an unattended screen stays protected.

  • Video appointments

    Whereby

    Private, encrypted video calls inside the system. Mums join from a link, with no app to download. Calls are not recorded unless you choose to.

  • Online payments

    Stripe

    Card, Apple Pay and Google Pay, with automatic receipts and easy refunds. Card details are handled by Stripe and never stored in your system. Money is paid straight into your bank account.

  • Reminders by email and text

    Email + SMS

    Automatic appointment reminders to cut down on missed visits. Messages never include health information.

  • Easy website editing

    Simple content editor

    Update your services, prices, team and news yourself, with no developer needed.

  • Hosting

    Vercel

    Keeps your website fast, online and on a secure connection (the padlock in the address bar), with updates going live smoothly.

Built with your responsibilities in mind

  • Designed around UK data protection law (UK GDPR), with patient data kept in the UK
  • Follows NHS digital clinical safety guidance for health record systems
  • Independently security-tested before launch
  • Records kept safely for the 25 years maternity records usually require

These are the tools we recommend today. If your needs change during planning, we’ll explain any changes in the same plain terms.
